Pixel Jam by Tront

Pixel Jam is a multiplayer prototype from a game jam, built in Unity and exported to WebGL so it runs in the browser with nothing to install.

It is kept online as a jam artefact rather than a finished game. By Tront (Trent Sterling), a contract network programmer and Unity generalist.
